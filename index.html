Max Offer solver

The asking price is $239,000. Your targets say $181,152.

Take the example duplex in the screenshots: 1418 Linden Ave, $2,500 a month in rent, 25% down at 7.00% on a 30-year loan. As a long-term rental it cash-flows $169 a month ($85 per door). The cap rate passes at 6.8%. Cash-on-cash is 2.4% against an 8% target, and the DSCR is 1.14x.

So what should you offer? The Max Offer tab solves for the highest purchase price that meets each of your targets. It uses a closed-form solve, not trial and error, and then re-computes each metric at that price so you can see it land on your target. For this deal, $181,152 meets all four rental targets, and cash-on-cash is the one that sets the limit.

It also gives the 70%-rule flip MAO and the max offer for the flip profit you want. That's a number to have before you call the agent.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

No. DoorMath is a standard .xlsx file. It uses only functions supported by Excel 2010 or newer, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc, and the last two are free. It was recalculated and tested in LibreOffice.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Every function in the workbook is one that Google Sheets supports, and the sensitivity grids use plain formulas instead of Excel Data Tables (which Google Sheets can't run). In Google Drive, choose New › File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets.

What about Apple Numbers?

Numbers hasn't been tested, so we don't claim it works there. On a Mac, use Excel, Google Sheets in your browser, or the free LibreOffice.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $29 once and keep the files. There's no account, no login and no renewal. Many deal-analysis apps charge a monthly subscription; this is a spreadsheet you own.

How do I get the file after purchase?

Shopify emails you a download link after checkout, and the link also appears on the order confirmation page. The download is one ZIP with the example workbook, a blank workbook, the Quick-Start Guide PDF, README, LICENSE and CHANGELOG.

Can I use it for clients?

The license covers your own analysis and the internal use of one business you own or work for. You can share printed or PDF outputs, like the Summary tab, with lenders, partners, clients or advisers. You can't resell, give away or publicly share the workbook files. If more than one business needs them, buy one license per business. The full terms are in LICENSE.txt.

Is this financial advice?

No. DoorMath is a calculator. The results depend entirely on the inputs you enter, and nothing in it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check your numbers with qualified professionals before you make a decision.

Does it handle taxes, depreciation or 1031 exchanges?

No. Income taxes, depreciation, capital-gains tax and 1031 exchanges are not modeled. Cash flow, IRR and profit figures are before tax.

Can I change the formulas?

Yes. Formula cells are locked with sheet protection so you don't overwrite them by accident, but there's no password. Unprotect the sheet (in Excel: Review › Unprotect Sheet) and edit whatever you like.

Where do I ask a question after buying?

Reply to your order confirmation email and include the version number (1.0.0).
